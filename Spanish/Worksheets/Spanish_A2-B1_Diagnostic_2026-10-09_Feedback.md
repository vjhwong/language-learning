## Grammar and vocabulary

1. **Cuando Clara llegó al muelle, los barcos ya no salían.** Use **llegó** for the completed arrival. **Llevaba** means “was carrying” or “used to carry”; it is a different verb. The background condition **no salían** is imperfect here.
2. Both answers are correct: **iba; decidió**. The first describes a usual summer habit; the second is a completed decision that Saturday.
3. **Esperaba** is correct. **Miraba** is also a natural answer here: “While she was waiting, she was looking at the map.” **Miró** would present looking at the map as a completed event. I would accept either interpretation.
4. **Al final, pasó una buena mañana porque descubrió un lugar nuevo.** Use **pasó** for the completed outcome. You chose the right verb for the second blank; spelling is **descubrió**.
5. Correct: **Aunque hacía mal tiempo, Clara salió de casa.**

The main pattern to practice is **imperfecto** for background, habits, and ongoing actions versus **pretérito** for completed events. You already used some imperfect forms well; the next step is making the contrast more reliable.
